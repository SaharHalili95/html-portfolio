## Direction contract

THESIS: Present an engineer the way Apple presents a product: one idea per screen, the real product as the hero material, quiet chrome. It refuses the résumé template: a sidebar with a photo, stacked white cards and tag pills.

OWN-WORLD: Apple product page. White and #f5f5f7 bands, ink #1d1d1f, secondary #6e6e73, one blue (#0071e3 buttons, #0066cc links). The platform face (SF on Apple devices) with tight negative tracking on large type. A floating translucent nav bar, pill buttons, "Learn more ›" text links, screenshots in large rounded frames with soft depth. Always light.

STORY: In one viewport the visitor knows who, what and how to reach him. Then each featured project gets its own chapter. More work, what he can build for you, about, and a contact close.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Glass nav (photo and name, sections, language, Contact pill). Centered headline at display scale, one line under it, primary pill "Let's work together" and "Download CV ›". Availability as a small status line. Below, the SalaryNet dashboard in a desktop frame with Linux Master on a phone overlapping it.

FORM: Apple product page, code-led. seed: none (unavailable)

Signature: chapters reveal on scroll (fade, rise and a slight scale on the screenshot, ease-out, once). The nav material thickens after scrolling. Buttons press to 0.97.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
