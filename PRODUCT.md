# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Recruiters and hiring team leads who open the link from a CV, LinkedIn or WhatsApp, usually on a phone, and decide in seconds whether to call.
- Potential clients (businesses and individuals) looking for someone to build a system, an app or a website, add AI to it, or maintain it.

Both want the same answer: this person is good at what he does and worth working with.

## Product Purpose

Sahar Halili's personal site. It markets him as a software developer who ships complete, working products, and turns a visit into contact: an interview for a role or a request for a project. Development is the focus; the site must not hurt his chances for any of the roles his CVs target.

## Positioning

Every project is live and opens in one tap, and the code backs every claim: tests in CI on real devices, security and accessibility work, bilingual Hebrew and English products. Most junior portfolios show tutorials; this one shows finished products.

## Operating Context

- Visitors arrive from a CV PDF, LinkedIn or WhatsApp; most on phones.
- Contact goes by email, phone, WhatsApp or LinkedIn. A CV PDF is downloadable from the site.
- Hosted on GitHub Pages at saharhalili95.github.io/html-portfolio, deployed by GitHub Actions on every push to main.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- React, TypeScript and Vite; HashRouter (in-page anchors must scroll, not change the route).
- English and Hebrew, with a language switch and full right-to-left layout in Hebrew.
- The Rambo pages (`/rambo`, `/pitbull-products`) are a separate awareness project and keep their own design.
- Facts on the site must match the code of each project and must not contradict any submitted CV. Military service is described only by what every CV version shares: IDF C4I Corps, system installation and troubleshooting, user permissions, secure data transfer.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Sahar Halili. He is a freelance developer and CS graduate with no full-time development job yet, looking for a first full-time role. The copy must never imply he has held one, and must not invent clients. The projects are the proof.
- A small photo of Sahar next to his name. A new photo may come later.
- Open to full-time roles and freelance projects, with full-time first.

## Evidence on Hand

- Live demos and public GitHub repositories for every listed project except NitaiBot (code only) and the Rambo site (internal page).
- Screenshots of six featured projects in `public/images/projects/`.
- CV PDF in `public/cv/Sahar_Halili_CV.pdf`.
- No testimonials, client names or employment at a software company exist; never invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Prove, don't claim: show the products, link the code.
2. Every fact is checked against its project and the CVs.
3. Phone first: the first screen on a phone must say who, what and how to reach him.
4. One clear action: contact, for a role or a project.

## Voice

Write the way Sahar would say it to someone. Plain first-person sentences and concrete facts about what was built. No slogans, no staccato fragments, no "not just X" contrasts, no periods on headings, and no filler such as "end to end", "seamless" or "secure by default". Readers told the owner the earlier copy sounded AI-written.
